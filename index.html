Privacy Policy — Teeth Score

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Teeth Score is designed to collect as little as possible. Your logs (meals, oral-care actions, and scores) are stored on your device and, if you're signed into iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We, the developers, cannot see this data — it never reaches our servers, because we don't operate any.

Data we process: none on our own servers. Your entries stay on your device and in your private iCloud.
AI features: meal descriptions and daily suggestions are processed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device model. Nothing you type is sent to us or to any third party.
Payments: subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive only Apple's standard aggregate sales reports, never your payment details.
Analytics/tracking: we do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
Data deletion: delete the app and, if desired, remove its iCloud data from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
Children: the app is not directed at children under 13.
