Privacy Policy

For ChompIQ · Last updated: October 17, 2026

ChompIQ is built to collect as little as possible. Your data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud — it never reaches our servers, because we don't operate any. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Who we are

ChompIQ is provided by Connor Yanz ("we", "us"). You can reach us at hello.connory@gmail.com with any privacy questions.

What data the app handles, and where it lives

The app records the things you log — meals and drinks, oral-care actions (brushing, flossing, mouthwash, gum), and the daily scores it calculates from them. This information is stored on your device, and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit.

  • We cannot see this data. It is stored locally and in your personal iCloud container. We have no servers and no ability to access, read, or retrieve it.
  • iCloud sync is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and iCloud terms, not by us.

AI features run on your device

When you use the "describe it" feature or receive a daily suggestion, the text is processed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device model (the Foundation Models framework). What you type is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is not used to train any model.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details. We receive only Apple's standard, aggregate sales and subscription reports, which do not identify you personally. Payment handling is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information. (We have none to sell.)

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device and in your iCloud, you are in control of it:

  • Delete individual entries at any time inside the app.
  • Delete the app to remove its local data from that device.
  • Remove synced data from iCloud via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your Apple device.

Children's privacy

ChompIQ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. (In any case, we do not collect personal information from any user on our own systems.)

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Because we do not hold your personal data on any system we control, the most direct way to exercise these rights is to manage or delete your data on your device and in your iCloud as described above. For data that Apple processes (iCloud sync, purchases), please contact Apple. You may also email us at hello.connory@gmail.com and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data — for example, if a future version introduces a feature that sends data off your device — we will update this policy and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello.connory@gmail.com.

This policy reflects the app's on-device / iCloud architecture as of the date above. It is provided as a starting point and is not legal advice; consider having it reviewed by a lawyer before publishing, particularly given the health-adjacent nature of the app.
