Why it's different

It's not just sugar. It's how often.

Every time you eat or drink, acid works on your enamel for a while after. So grazing all day is harder on your teeth than the same food in one sitting. ChompIQ is built around that — it rewards spacing things out and cleaning up in time, not just eating less.
