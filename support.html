What is the protection score?

It's a daily 0–100 estimate of how well you protected your teeth from cavity risk, based on what you logged — meals and drinks, and oral-care actions like brushing, flossing, mouthwash, and gum. Higher is better.

Why did my score drop?

Eating and drinking triggers acid activity that's hard on enamel, and doing it frequently through the day adds up faster than one sitting. Brushing in time, flossing, xylitol gum, and rinsing all help bring the score back up.

Do the AI features need internet?

No. The "describe it" feature and your daily suggestion run entirely on your device. They require an Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone; on other devices you'll log with the tile buttons and get a quick tip instead.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and synced through your own private iCloud. We have no servers and can't see your data. To remove synced data, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
